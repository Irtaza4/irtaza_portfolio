Overview

B2B Cabs is a dual-app corporate transportation system built to automate employee pick-ups and drop-offs to and from the office. It consists of two separate Flutter apps — a Driver App and a Company App — giving businesses full control over employee transit while keeping drivers coordinated in real time.

The platform eliminates the chaos of manual scheduling by providing a centralized system where companies manage routes, employees, and vehicles, while drivers get instant dispatch updates and live navigation.

The Challenge

Corporate employee transportation is logistically complex — multiple shifts, varying pickup locations, driver availability, and last-minute changes all need to be handled seamlessly. Traditional methods rely on phone calls and spreadsheets, causing delays and inefficiencies that impact employee productivity.

The Solution

Two dedicated apps working in sync. The Company App lets businesses create routes, assign employees, and monitor fleet status in real time. The Driver App handles dispatch notifications, turn-by-turn navigation, and live location sharing — all powered by Foreground Services to ensure continuous background tracking even when the app is minimized.

Key Features

Company App

Customized Routes: Tailored pickup/drop-off routes to reduce travel time.
Employee Scheduling: Easy scheduling with flexibility for last-minute changes.
Fleet Overview: Centralized dashboard to monitor all active vehicles.
Cost Management: Affordable, scalable solutions for any business size.

Driver App

Instant Dispatch: FCM & OneSignal push notifications for real-time job alerts.
Live Tracking: Foreground Services for continuous background location sharing.
Navigation: Google Maps API integration for turn-by-turn route guidance.
Secure Rides: Verified driver profiles for passenger peace of mind.

Technical Highlights

Foreground Services for uninterrupted background location tracking on Android.
Google Maps API for real-time vehicle tracking and route navigation.
FCM & OneSignal for instant driver dispatch and status push notifications.
Firebase Firestore and Auth for data sync and secure user management.
Dual App Architecture — separate Flutter apps sharing a common backend.
Published on both App Store and Google Play Store for Driver & Company apps.
