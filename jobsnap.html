JobSnap

Local Home Services, In a Snap

June 2025 – Nov 2025 iOS & Android Live on App Store & Play Store
Flutter Firebase Node.js WebSockets OneSignal Provider REST APIs

Overview

JobSnap is a cross-platform marketplace app that connects customers needing local home services with trusted service providers in their area — fast, fair, and frustration-free. Whether you need yard work, appliance repair, snow removal, or house cleaning, JobSnap gets it done without middlemen or markups.

The platform is built around two distinct user roles — Customers and Service Providers — with a 3-tier role-based access control system (User, Provider, Agent) ensuring every interaction is secure and well-structured.

The Problem

Finding reliable local home service workers has always been painful. Existing platforms take heavy per-lead fees from providers, charge customers inflated prices, and drown both sides in middlemen. Service providers lose control of their income and schedule. Customers can't trust who's showing up.

The Solution

JobSnap cuts out the chaos. Customers post any job in seconds — snap a photo, add a description, go live. Providers browse real jobs in their area, express interest, and get hired fast. No per-lead fees, no commissions. Providers pay one flat monthly price and keep 100% of what they earn.

Technical Highlights

Clean Architecture with modular Flutter codebase for scalable feature growth.
WebSockets for real-time task bidding and live in-app messaging.
3-Tier RBAC — User, Provider, and Agent roles with scoped permissions.
Firebase + Node.js backend for auth, data, and business logic.
OneSignal push notifications for job alerts, bids, and messages.
Provider state management for reactive and performant UI updates.
