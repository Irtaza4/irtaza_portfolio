Ride Hoppy is a two-sided mobile application solution designed to connect passengers with corporate employees who are willing to offer free rides during their daily commute.

The Challenge

For many corporate employees, the daily commute is a significant source of stress and expense. Public transport can be unreliable, while ride-hailing services become prohibitively expensive.

The Solution

Ride Hoppy bridges this gap by creating a trusted, internal ride-sharing network. Rides are free, fostering a culture of mutual support.

The system consists of two distinct interfaces: the Driver App and the Rider App.

Core Experience

For Drivers

Route & Schedule: Easily set daily commute routes and available timings.
Verification: Corporate email verification ensures safety.

For Riders

Smart Matching: Find drivers passing near your location instantly.
Live Tracking: Real-time map tracking for your ride.

Project Impact & Statistics
