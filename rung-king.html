Rung King

Classic South Asian Card Game — Online & vs AI

Feb 2026 – May 2026 iOS & Android 2D Multiplayer Card Game
Flutter Flame Engine WebSockets Dart Provider AI Opponents

Overview

Rung King brings the classic South Asian card game Rung (also known as Court Piece or Hidden Rung) to your phone — played the way it's meant to be played. Built with Flutter and the Flame Engine for smooth 2D card animations, the game features real-time online multiplayer via WebSockets and smart AI opponents for offline play.

Challenge real players worldwide or practice your strategy against intelligent computer opponents. Pick your trump suit, outplay your rivals, and climb to the top.

Features

Multiplayer

Online Multiplayer: Play live matches with real players in real time via WebSockets.
2v2 Team Play: Partner up with a teammate and dominate as a duo.
Trump Selection: Strategic Rung (trump) calling just like the real game.

Solo Play

Play vs AI: Practice offline against intelligent computer opponents.
Smooth Animations: Fluid card mechanics powered by Flame Engine.
Coin Betting: Select stake before each game — win and take it all.

How to Play

Rung is a trick-taking card game played in teams of 2. One player calls the trump suit (Rung), and both teams compete to win the most tricks. The team that wins 7 tricks first wins the round.

01

Form Teams

Two players pair up against the other two in a 2v2 format.

02

Call Trump

One player strategically calls the trump suit (Rung) to gain the edge.

03

Win Tricks

Play cards to win tricks — the team with 7 tricks wins the round.

04

Earn Coins

Win coins from your opponents. Lose and you pay up. Grow your stack.

Simple to learn, hard to master — just like the real thing.

Coin System

Every match has stakes. Select your coin bet before each game — win and you take it all, lose and you pay up. Earn coins by winning matches and grow your stack as you climb the ranks. Whether you play it safe or go all in, every hand counts.

Technical Highlights

Flame Engine for 2D game rendering, card animations, and game loop management.
WebSockets for low-latency real-time multiplayer gameplay.
AI Opponent Logic built in Dart for smart offline gameplay decisions.
Flutter + Dart cross-platform codebase for iOS and Android.
Trick-taking engine with full Rung rules — trump logic, team scoring, and round management.
Coin economy with persistent balances and per-match stake selection.
